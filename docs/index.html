🌦️ Weather Poem 🌦️

Comparing Bosnia & Denmark
🇧🇦 🇩🇰

🌡️ Weather Tomorrow

🇧🇦 Bosnia: 36.9°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 22.3°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Aalborg: 21.3°C 🌬️
🇬🇧 English
Bosnia's heat abides, a sweltering day, 36.9 degrees, no clouds in sight to stay, Aalborg's breeze whispers, a gentle hue, While Copenhagen's calm, a soothing dew, Tomorrow, Aalborg's cool breeze I'd find, A comfortable day, where I'd leave my mind, The rain falls softly, a gentle kiss so fair, Aalborg's charm beckons, beyond compare.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
Bosnu goriću, dan topli i dug, 36,9 stupanj, bez oblaka koji bi stajali dug, Aalborga zanjuce, lagano, lijep, A Copenhagenova mirisa, lagana kiša, Ujutru u Aalborgu hladnejima će me zaneti, Prirodno doživljaj, kad ću umom pustiti, Kiša mučkom da se klanja, laganom zanosu, Aalborgu lepotu zove, iznad svega drugoga.
