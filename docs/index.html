🌦️ Weather Poem 🌦️

Comparing Bosnia & Denmark
🇧🇦 🇩🇰

🌡️ Weather Tomorrow

🇧🇦 Bosnia: 33.0°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 23.4°C 🌬️
🇩🇰 Aalborg: 26.9°C 🌬️
🇬🇧 English
Where sun shines bright in Bosnia's land, Temperature soars, a warm command, No wind to blow, a gentle sigh, A dry and hot day, passing by. Where sea meets sky in Copenhagen's sway, Wind whispers through, a gentle play, A cooler breeze, a welcome friend, A day of relaxation, till the end. And Aalborg's warmth, a gentle heat, Temperature and wind, a perfect beat, A city waits, with open hearts, Aalborg's charm, a work of art. But if I chose, where I'd love to be, Tomorrow's forecast, a perfect sea, In Aalborg's warmth, I'd love to stay, The nicest of three, a perfect day. Samo na jugu Bosne dade toplina, Temperature se uzdiše, hladnog zapravo, Bežička brizga, mirna zanosa, Suočev dan prolazi, bez kape. U Danskoj kopenhagenu vetar je hlad, A temperatura se svaljuje u hladnu zanu, Neobično hladan dan, u svakom slučaju, Dobar dan proveden, na svaki način. Aalborg se zadržava u toplom svetlu, Temperatura i vetar pružaju savršen ritam, Grad čeka, sa otvorenim srcama, Aalborgova ljepota, savršeno djelo. Ali ako izaberem, gdje bi volio stati, Izađimo u sutra, savršena mala plošćadi. U Aalborgu će se voljeti biti i sutra, Najljepši od tri, savršeni dan.
