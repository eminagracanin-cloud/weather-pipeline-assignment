🌦️ Weather Poem 🌦️

Comparing Bosnia & Denmark
🇧🇦 🇩🇰

🌡️ Weather Tomorrow

🇧🇦 Bosnia: 26.9°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 20.8°C 🌬️
🇩🇰 Aalborg: 20.6°C 🌬️
🇬🇧 English
Under the Bosnian sun so bright, Temperature high, a gentle night, No rain, no wind, just calm and fair, A perfect day, without a single care. But in Copenhagen's winds do blow and sway, Twenty-three knots, a breezy day, No rain, no cloud, just sea and blue, A pleasant scene, but not for me or you. Aalborg's winds, though milder be, A windy day, with a slight gale, No rain, no cloud, just a windy sea, A place to visit, but not for me. Tomorrow I would choose to be, In Bosnia, where the sun shines free, A warm and calm, and peaceful night, A perfect place, a perfect sight.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
Pod bosanskim sunčevim svjetlom, Temperatura visoka, tiha noć, Ni kiše, ni vjetar, samo tiha i sjajna, Perfectan dan bez jedne brige. Ali u Kopenhagenskim vjetrovima valja i valja, Dvadeset trinaest metara, bljesava dnevno, Ni kiša, ni oblak, samo more i more, Slatka scena, ali za mene ni ona stvar. Aalborgov vjetar,ako i blago teče, Vjetreni dan, malo jakog vjetra, Ni kiša, ni oblak, samo vjetar i more, Mjesto za posjet, ali za mene nije ono. Sutra bih volio biti, U Bosni, gdje sunce svjetli slobodno, Ljubavna i tiha noć, Perfectno mjesto, perfectan pogled.
