🌦️ Weather Poem 🌦️

Comparing Bosnia & Denmark
🇧🇦 🇩🇰

🌡️ Weather Tomorrow

🇧🇦 Bosnia: 32.0°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 23.3°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Aalborg: 27.0°C ☀️
🇬🇧 English
Bosnia's heat is fierce and strong, 32 degrees where the sun belongs, No rain to cool the summer's sway, Just wind to whisper through the day. In Copenhagen's calm and gentle breeze, The temperature's a pleasant ease, 23 degrees a perfect time to roam, But Aalborg's 27 is a sweeter home. The weather's warm in all three places, But if I had to choose, I'd gaze, Towards Copenhagen's gentle delight, A perfect day to step into its light.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
Bosna je vruća i snažna, 32 stepena gdje se suncu pripada, Ni kiša koja bi hladiila ljetu, Ravničarski vjetar koji šapće kroz dan. U Kopenhagenu tihom i mirnom vetru, Temperatura je ugodan pohod, 23 stepena ugodno vrijeme za hod, Aalborgov 27 je lijepa kuća. Vrijeme je toplije u svim tri mesta, Ali ako moram izabrati, pogledat ću, prema Kopenhagenu tihoj razonodi, Pravno vrijeme da stupim u nju.
