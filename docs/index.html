🌦️ Weather Poem 🌦️

Comparing Bosnia & Denmark
🇧🇦 🇩🇰

🌡️ Weather Tomorrow

🇧🇦 Bosnia: 21.0°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 16.6°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Aalborg: 18.3°C ☀️
🇬🇧 English
Warmth descends over Bosnia's land, 21 degrees, a gentle hand. A gentle breeze stirs the air, While others may feel the chill there. Copenhagen's chill begins to break, But wind and rain begin to shake, The city's calm, a quiet hue, A beauty unique, both old and new. Aalborg's coast, a gentle breeze, 18 degrees, a warm summer ease, The wind whispers secrets in the night, A soothing calm, a peaceful sight. But where to be tomorrow to share, A warmth that's found in Bosnia's air, The gentle breeze, the calm serene, A perfect place to be, so green.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
Šarmantu toplina pada preko Bosne, 21 stepen, blagoćudna ruka. Blagoćudni vetar izbacuje zrak, Pa druge često osjećaju hlad. Kopenhagenska hladnost počinje primiti, Pa vjetar i kiša počinju se pokretati, Gradski mir, tiha boja, Lepota jedinstvena, stara i nova. Aalborgovoj obali, blago vetra, 18 stepeni, toplina leteća, Vjetar šapuće tajne u noć, Mirnoća tiha, miran pogled. Ali gdje biti sutra podijeliti, Toplu koju je Bosna, Blagoćudni vetar, tiha umiljost, Lepo mjesto biti zeleni.
