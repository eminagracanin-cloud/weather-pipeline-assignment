🌦️ Weather Poem 🌦️

Comparing Bosnia & Denmark
🇧🇦 🇩🇰

🌡️ Weather Tomorrow

🇧🇦 Bosnia: 32.4°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 26.0°C ☀️
🇩🇰 Aalborg: 31.4°C ☀️
🇬🇧 English
Bosnia's heat beats down with fervent might, While Copenhagen and Aalborg feel cooler in the light. Aalborg's breeze at eighteen miles an hour does play, But which of these towns has the most pleasant day? No rain to ruin the fun, just sunshine all around, But Copenhagen's twenty-six degrees makes me want to be found In Denmark's pleasant streets, where sea meets shore so fine, Tomorrow I'd love to be in Copenhagen, all the time.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
Bosna zagrlja toplom snažno, Kopenhagen i Aalborg osjećaju hladnije u svjetlu. Brušulj Aalborga igra se brzinom od osamnaest milja u sat, Ali ko od ovih gradova ima najlepše jutro? Ni jedan kap kišnice ne bi rušila igru, No jedanestnaest kila u satu sa Bosne ne bi me uvjerila, Kopenhagenov dvadeseti šesto znači, da bih se mogla naći, U Danskoj lijepim ulicama gdje more srete obalu, svak'put.
