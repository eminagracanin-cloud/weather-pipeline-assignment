Comparing Bosnia & Denmark
🇧🇦 🇩🇰

🌡️ Weather Tomorrow

🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 22.9°C 🌬️
🇩🇰 Aalborg: 21.4°C 🌬️
🇬🇧 English
Under sizzling Bosnian skies so bright, A heat wave sweeps, a sweltering night. Four point six millimeters fall and fade, As heat beats on, the land is made. Copenhagen's calm in cooler air, Twenty two point nine, a gentle care. No rain to fall, just wind to sway, A peaceful night, at end of day. Aalborg too, a tranquil scene, Twenty one point four, a warm breeze serene. No rain to speak, a dry and clear sky, A night to rest, without goodbye. But where to go, if I could choose, To be tomorrow, with no dispute. I'd flee to Copenhagen's gentle grasp, A soothing night, in peaceful clasp.

Pod suncem Bosne svjetlo, svrločno dani, Intenzivna vatra, noć bez sanj. Četiri šestina milimetra padnu i nestane, Kada se toplota vrti, zemlja se oživi. Kopenhagenska tišina, u hladnoći zraka, Dvadesetdvogodišni, miran umak. Ni kiše nikad, samo vjetar vala, Mirno noćenje, u kraju dana. Albertov scen, miran kraj, Dvadeset jedanaest godina, miran zrak. Ni kiša ni reći, suvo i čisto nebo, Najamno noćenje bez razgovora. Ali gdje ići, ako mogu izabrati, Biću sutra, bez ikakvih pitanja. Bježi sam u Kopenhagensko umiljstvo, Umirujuće noćenje u umiljenu objemu.
