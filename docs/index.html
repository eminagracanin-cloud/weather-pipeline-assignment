Comparing Bosnia & Denmark

🇬🇧 English
Under Bosnia's warm sun so bright, A gentle breeze on a cloudless night, No drop of rain to dampen the day, A perfect spot to play, come what may. Copenhagen's wind whispers through the trees, A cooler climate in the summer breeze, But rainless days mean not a chance to see, Summer blooms in full ecstasy. Aalborg's wind whispers secrets all night, Crisp and cool, a summer delight, But like its friends, no rain to play with or share, A cloudless sky, a lovely lair. But if I had to pick the place to be, Tomorrow I'd choose to be in Bosnia free, With temperatures high and skies so divine, No need for more, just this weather fine.
