Comparing Bosnia & Denmark

🇬🇧 English
Under Bosnia's warm summer sky, Temperatures climb, winds softly sigh, A gentle rain falls, just a tease, 24.3 degrees, a pleasant breeze. Under Copenhagen's chilly, rain-soaked air, Gusts of wind howl, rain falls everywhere, 10.9 degrees, a soggy, grey day, Dreary and cold, come what may. Under Aalborg's sun-kissed, wind-swept shore, Temperatures rise, not a drop to store, 13.1 degrees, a gentle, calm sight, A perfect day, free from the night. If I could choose where to be tomorrow's delight, Aalborg's sunshine, with no rain in sight, Temperatures just right, not too hot or cold, A perfect day, young and old.

Pod aalborgovim suncem i vetrom, Temperatura pada, ni nikada voda u zalihama, 13.1 stepena, sav jetno i mirno, Perfektni dan, slobodan od tame. Ako moću izabirati gdje budem sutra večer, Aalborgov suncem i bez kiše, sve je kao sviđa, Temperatura upadne, nije previše vruće ili hladno, Perfektni dan, mlad i star.
