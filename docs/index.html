Comparing Bosnia & Denmark

🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 21.7°C 🌬️

🇬🇧 English
Bosnia's heat beats down so bright, A scorching 29, with nary a sight, Of rain or cloud, just clear blue space, A hot and dry day, a burning pace. Copenhagen's cool, a gentle breeze, 21.7 degrees, a pleasant squeeze, With a sprinkle of rain, just enough to bring, A refreshing feeling, as its people sing. Aalborg's chill, with wind in its hair, 18.5 degrees, with nothing to spare, No rain to come, just a dry, crisp air, A brisk and invigorating, morning share.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
Bosna svjetla i vruća dana, 29,2 stepena bez kiše, bez zorene, Samo prozračnost, samo toplina, Svuda vrućina, svuda spaljivost dna. Kopenhagen cool i miren dah, 21,7 stepeni, s malo kiše, malo sna, Dobili od kiše što je dovoljno, Da donese hladnu osjećajnu. Aalborg hladno i vjetar u kosi, 18,5 stepeni, bez kiše, bez sna, Samo suhe i krhke zrake zraka, Hladni i zdravljiva utjeha.
