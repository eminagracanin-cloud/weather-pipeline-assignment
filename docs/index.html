Comparing Bosnia & Denmark

🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 17.7°C ☀️

🇬🇧 English
In Bosnia's warmth, the day unwinds free Twenty-two degrees, a gentle breeze to be A drop of rain, a hint of summer's sway But wind whispers of a fresher day. In Copenhagen, cool and calm does lie Seventeen degrees, a peaceful sky No rain to dampen, just a gentle breeze that blows A Danish day, with no cause for woes. In Aalborg, similar, yet distinct does shine Seventeen and a half, the winds does entwine Dry and bright, as morning sun does rise A Danish haven, where worries dies.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
U biH-u vrućina se izvukla slobodno Dvadeset jedna stepenica, lagano vjetrenjače Kapići padaju kao znak ljetovalnog vala Ali u vetru tiho predosjećavaju drugačiji dan. U Kopenhagenu hladno i tihko izgleda Sedamnaest stepeni, miran nebeski svod Ni kiša da bi zasmetala, samo lagano puha vjetar Kopenhagenski dan bez razloga žalosti. U Alborgu slično, ali drugačije sjaji Sedamnaest i pol stepeni, vjetar se ukršta Suho i svjetlo kao što se rano izdiže mornaring sunce Dansko utočište gdje se brige gube.
