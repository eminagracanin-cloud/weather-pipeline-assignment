In Copenhagen's harbor, wind gusts strong and free, While rain falls lightly, just enough for you and me, A pleasant day with temperature at its peak, A mix of weather, both calm and unique. But let's compare these cities, side by side, Bosnia's heat, Copenhagen's wind, Aalborg's gentle tide, Aalborg's weather's quite refreshing, don't you see, It would be nice to be in Aalborg tomorrow. Bosna, gdje topel suncu sijeku 36 stepeni, Suhog vetra tiho, sa 9 i sedam kilometara u sat, Ni kap poći da olazi, bez jedne kapljice kiše, Topao dan koji ostavi suhi zvuk. Ali da poredimo ove gradske ljepote, Bosna, hladno vjetre Kopenhagen, Aalborg, Aalborg budi prijatna vremenska situacija, Bi bio lijep da bismo u Aalborgu sutra.
