In Bosnia, the sun shines bright and bold, 27 degrees, with winds that gently unfold. No clouds are seen, the sky is clear, A perfect day, without a single fear. In Copenhagen, the wind whispers low, 38.9 km/h, a gentle breeze, don't you know? The temperature's cool, at 17 degrees, Not bad for a day, with a hint of breeze. In Aalborg, the wind whispers too, 39.6 km/h, but the sun is shining through. At 19 degrees, it's a lovely sight, A beautiful day, full of joy and light. If I had to choose, I'd say it's nice, To be in Bosnia, where the sun shines in its eyes. Warm and clear, with not a cloud in sight, A perfect day to bask, in the warm, golden light.

U Bosni svetlosti i sunce sjaji, 27 stupnjeva, vjetar koji mirno plavaljenje. Ni truha u vidu, orah koji je razgledati, Izvrstan dan, bez jedne strahu. U Kopenhagenu vjetar šapta niz, 38.9 km/h, lagan zrak koji, znao da nije. Temperatura hladno, u stupnjeva 17, Dobar dan za, što znači malo zraka. U Aalborgu, vjetaru se šapta i vjetare, 39.6, brzo, dok se ne gleda. U stupnjeva 19, lijep je, nešto što Prekrasan dan, pun sreće i svjetlosti. Ako trebam izabrati, rekoh, da bi bilo ljepše, Bi u Bosni, gdje sunce sjaji u oči. Toplo i jasno, ni jedan oblak nije u vidu, Izvrstan dan da se baste, u svetlom zlatnom svjetlu.
