Comparing Bosnia & Denmark

🇬🇧 English
Warm Bosnia's sun beats down so bright, Copenhagen's breeze whispers a cooler night, Aalborg's heat rivals Bosnia's gentle sway, But rain-free nights make them both a better stay. Tomorrow I'd like to be in Aalborg's heat, A perfect day to walk by the calm sea's beat.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
Bosansko sunce svijeća toplu noć, Kopenhagenovo blago vjetreno plače, Aalborgova topla svijeća tako blaga, No noć bez kiše čini ih sve boljim mjestom. Nakon sutra želim biti u Aalborgovu toploj topline, Idealan dan za šetnju uz tiho moru.
