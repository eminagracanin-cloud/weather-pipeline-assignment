Comparing Bosnia & Denmark

🇬🇧 English
Bosnia basks in the sun's warm rays, Heat of thirty-two degrees on display. Wind whispers low at a gentle pace, Calm skies above with no single drop in its place. Copenhagen's cooler breeze invites a stroll, Eighteen degrees, with rain sprinkling below. Gusts of wind blow with a fierce might, Grey skies weep, a dismal sight. Aalborg's winds whip with a force so bold, Thirty-five kilometers, the gusts unfold. A drizzle falls with a light caress, Grey skies weep, like Copenhagen's distress. But where would I like to be tomorrow? Bosnia warms my heart with its sun-kissed glow, A perfect haven from the northern cold.
