Comparing Bosnia & Denmark
🇧🇦 🇩🇰

🌡️ Weather Tomorrow

🇩🇰 Copenhagen: 24.2°C ☀️

🇬🇧 English
Under Bosnia's scorching sun so bright, Where heat swells, a sweltering night. The winds that blow, with gentle might, A stillness falls, no drop in sight. Tomorrow, a choice so fair, Aalborg's breeze, with warmth to spare, Copenhagen's calm, a haven rare, Where the heart finds solace, with love to share.
🇧🇦 Bosanski
Pod Bosnom vruću suncem toliko svjetlo, Gdje toplina stvara vruću noć. Vjetar koji puše, sa kukavičkom snagom, Padne tišina, bez jedne kapljice. Sutra, takva izbor tako savršen, Aalborgov dah, s toplinom za vrijeme, Kopenhagenska mirisna mira, rijetka džamija, Gdje srcu pronađe utočište, s ljubavi koja se dijeli.
